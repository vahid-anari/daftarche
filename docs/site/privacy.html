Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Daftarche is made by one person, Vahid Anari, and runs on your own devices. There is no Daftarche server, no account with us, no analytics and no advertising. We never see your data.

On your Mac

Your transactions, categories, rules and settings are kept in a folder on your own computer (on a Mac: Library/Application Support/Daftarche in your home folder), together with automatic backups. Nothing in it is sent anywhere, except the phone copy described below, and only when you connect a phone.

Google Drive, when you connect a phone

If you choose Sign In with Google in Daftarche's Settings, Daftarche asks Google for permission to see and change only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission) and for your email address, which it shows in Settings so you know which account is connected. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

For each phone you connect, Daftarche creates one file in your Drive, named Daftarche phone - <the phone's name>, and keeps it up to date with a copy of what the phone shows (accounts, balances, categories, transactions, tags and loans; never bank account numbers or import rules). Before the copy leaves your Mac it is encrypted with a random key made for that phone. The key is only in the QR code shown on your Mac; it is never sent to Google or to anyone else, so the file in your Drive cannot be read by Google or by anyone who finds it. The file is shared as "anyone with the link" so that your phone can download it without signing in to Google.

Disconnect in Daftarche's Settings deletes that phone's file. Sign Out removes Daftarche's access to your Drive; you can also remove it at any time in your Google Account (Security › Your connections to third-party apps & services). The sign-in and the phones' keys are kept on your Mac only, in a file only your user account can read.

Daftarche's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from your Google account is used only to keep your own phone's copy up to date. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, not read by people, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

On your phone

The phone app downloads its file from your Drive, opens it with its key and keeps the last copy in its own storage so it works without a connection. It never sends anything back. It checks GitHub once a day for a newer version of the app (GitHub sees that a request was made, nothing more).
